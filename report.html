Share Your Case

You don't need documents, dates, or the “right” words to begin. Tell us what happened in your own way — write as much or as little as you feel able to. Only your email is required.

Before you begin:
  • Nothing is published automatically. Every submission is reviewed by our team, and nothing goes public without your approval.
  • We anonymize first. Before anything is published we remove names, dates, and any detail that could identify you or your child.
  • You choose what may be public using the consent checkboxes at the end. Everything is private by default.
  • Your email is used only to verify your submission and contact you — it is never published.
  • Photos and documents are optional. Please redact anything (addresses, phone numbers, financial details) you don't want us to see.
